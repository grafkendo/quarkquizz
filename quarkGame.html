Physics Subatomic Model Quiz

Standard Model of Particle Physics

First Generation
Up
Quark
+⅔
Up Quark: One of the two lightest quarks. Together with down quarks, they make up protons and neutrons. Mass: ~2.2 MeV/c². Charge: +⅔. Discovered in 1968 at SLAC.
Down
Quark
-⅓
Down Quark: The other first-generation quark. Essential component of nuclear matter. Mass: ~4.7 MeV/c². Charge: -⅓. Forms stable matter with up quarks.
Electron
-1
Electron: The lightest charged lepton. Responsible for chemical bonding and electricity. Mass: 0.511 MeV/c². Charge: -1. First discovered subatomic particle (1897).
Electron
Neutrino
0
Electron Neutrino: Nearly massless particle that rarely interacts with matter. Mass: <2.2 eV/c². Charge: 0. Involved in beta decay and solar fusion.
Second Generation
Charm
Quark
+⅔
Charm Quark: Second-generation quark discovered in 1974. Mass: ~1.28 GeV/c². Charge: +⅔. Found in various hadrons and mesons.
Strange
Quark
-⅓
Strange Quark: Named for its unexpectedly long lifetime. Mass: ~95 MeV/c². Charge: -⅓. Found in K-mesons and other exotic particles.
Muon
-1
Muon: A heavier version of the electron. Important in cosmic rays. Mass: 105.7 MeV/c². Charge: -1. Mean lifetime: 2.2 microseconds.
Muon
Neutrino
0
Muon Neutrino: Second-generation neutrino, discovered in 1962. Mass: <0.17 MeV/c². Charge: 0. Created in pion decay.
Third Generation
Top
Quark
+⅔
Top Quark: The heaviest known elementary particle. Mass: ~173 GeV/c². Charge: +⅔. Discovered in 1995 at Fermilab.
Bottom
Quark
-⅓
Bottom Quark: Also called the beauty quark. Mass: ~4.18 GeV/c². Charge: -⅓. Important in B-meson physics.
Tau
-1
Tau: The heaviest lepton. Discovered in 1975. Mass: 1.777 GeV/c². Charge: -1. Extremely short lifetime.
Tau
Neutrino
0
Tau Neutrino: The last lepton to be discovered (2000). Mass: <18.2 MeV/c². Charge: 0. Produced in tau lepton decay.
Force Carriers (Bosons)
Photon
γ
Photon: Carrier of electromagnetic force. Massless particle of light. Mass: 0. Charge: 0. Speed: c (speed of light). No rest frame.
Gluon
g
Gluon: Carrier of the strong nuclear force. Mass: 0. Charge: 0. Comes in 8 types. Binds quarks together.
W/Z
Bosons
W/Z Bosons: Carriers of the weak nuclear force. W± Mass: 80.4 GeV/c². Z Mass: 91.2 GeV/c². Mediate radioactive decay.
Higgs
Boson
Higgs Boson: Gives other particles their mass. Mass: 125 GeV/c². Discovered in 2012 at CERN. Completes Standard Model.
